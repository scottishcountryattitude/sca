Wee Cabmate terms of use and disclaimer

Last updated 21 November 2026

Please read this before using Wee Cabmate. The app is an aid to help you through your shift. It does not replace your tachograph, your operator's systems, a proper walkaround check, a truck sat nav or your own judgement. You are always responsible for driving safely and legally.

Drivers' hours and rest

Wee Cabmate does not replace your tachograph. The timers, breaks, rest and "rest owed" figures are estimates to help you plan. Automatic start and stop uses GPS and can start or stop late, or miss time, for example with poor signal or if your phone's battery saving stops it. Always go by your tachograph and the official rules. You are responsible for keeping to drivers' hours, working time and rest rules and your employer's policies.

Daily checks and defects

The daily checks list is a reminder based on DVSA walkaround check guidance. It does not replace a full walkaround check or your operator's defect reporting system. Report defects the way your operator requires. A record in the app is not a legal defect report unless your operator has agreed to use it as one.

Low bridges

Bridge information comes from public and third-party sources and may be incomplete, out of date or wrong. Warnings depend on GPS and the vehicle height you entered, and may be late or missed. Always know your vehicle's height, obey road signs and plan your route. Never rely on the app alone to avoid a bridge strike.

Truck parking and places of interest

Truck parking and places of interest come from public data and from other drivers. They may be wrong, closed, full or unsuitable for your vehicle. Places marked "not verified" have not yet been confirmed by other drivers. Check that a place is safe and suitable before you use it.

Sat nav and links

Buttons that open a sat nav, maps or website take you to other companies' apps and sites, which we do not control. Use a truck sat nav set to your vehicle's dimensions.

Do not use the app while driving

Set the app up before you move off and only use it when you are safely parked. It is illegal to hold or use a phone while driving.

Rules and summaries

The rules summaries in the app are a quick guide only. GOV.UK is the official source, and rules can change.
