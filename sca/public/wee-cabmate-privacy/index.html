Wee Cabmate privacy policy

Last updated 21 November 2026

In short: almost everything you put into Wee Cabmate stays on your own phone, and we never see it. The only thing that leaves your phone is a place of interest that you choose to add, confirm or report, which is shared with other drivers. No adverts, and we never sell your data.

Who we are

Wee Cabmate is an app for HGV drivers made by Scottish Country Attitude, Scotland. We are responsible for the small amount of data described below. Contact us at scottishcountryattitude@outlook.com.

What stays on your phone

This is stored only on your phone. It is not sent to us and we cannot see it:

  • your user ID and PIN
  • your driver details: document references and expiry dates (driving licence, digital tachograph card, CPC, ADR card, petroleum driver passport) and terminal inductions
  • the vehicles you add (registration, type, weight and height)
  • your shifts: start and end times, daily checks, defect answers and reasons, PPE checks, delays, yard duties, end-of-shift notes and your diary
  • your driving time, breaks and rest records

Deleting the app removes all of this from your phone.

Your location

Wee Cabmate uses your phone's location to:

  • start and stop your driving timer automatically, but only if you switch on automatic mode. This needs location "all the time" so it keeps working when the app is in the background, and your phone shows a notification while it is on. You can switch it off at any time on the Drive tab.
  • warn you about low bridges and show nearby truck parking and places of interest
  • fill in the position of a place of interest when you add one

Your location is used on your phone and is not sent to us or stored by us. The only exception is the position of a place you choose to add.

What is shared: places of interest

When you add, confirm or report a place of interest (for example a snack bar or layby), we save what you entered (name, type, road, direction, notes, and whether it suits artics), its map position and the time. Every Wee Cabmate user can see it. It is saved against a random anonymous ID that the app creates for your phone, not your name, phone number or email address. Please do not put anyone's personal details in a place's notes. You can delete places you have added.

Companies that help us run the app

  • Google Firebase (Google) stores the shared places of interest and creates the anonymous ID. The data is stored in Europe.
  • Expo (650 Industries) delivers app updates. When the app checks for an update, Expo receives standard technical information such as your IP address, device type and app version.
  • Google Play distributes the app.

If you use a button that opens another app or website (a sat nav, maps, a phone call, GOV.UK or a truck stop's website), that app or site's own privacy policy applies.

Why we can use this data

We share places of interest because you choose to submit them, and it is in drivers' legitimate interests to have a shared, up-to-date list. We use technical update information to keep the app working and secure.

How long we keep it

Data on your phone stays until you delete it or remove the app. Shared places stay in the list while they are useful, and are removed when the person who added them deletes them or drivers report them as gone.

Your rights

You can ask us for a copy of your data, or ask us to correct, delete or stop using it, by emailing scottishcountryattitude@outlook.com. Because places are saved against an anonymous ID, tell us the place's name and roughly where it is. If you are unhappy with how we handle your data, you can complain to the Information Commissioner's Office at ico.org.uk.

Children

Wee Cabmate is made for professional drivers and is not intended for anyone under 18.

Changes to this policy

If we change this policy, we will update the date at the top. If a change affects what leaves your phone, we will tell you in the app first.
